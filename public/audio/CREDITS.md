Mapping: cucumber and pickles use cucumber cutting; tomato uses tomato slicing; onion uses onion chopping; lettuce and cabbage use cabbage chopping; pepper, jalapeño, carrot and mushroom use vegetable chopping; chicken and beef use the chicken cutting recording; every sauce uses the ketchup squeeze recording. Cheese, grains and other cooking stages retain procedural foley. These are product-family sound effects, not separate recordings of every recipe variation.

Playback uses a single AudioContext and cached decoded clips. Gesture unlock starts loading. Until a clip is ready (or if it fails), procedural foley is used for that event, with no delayed playback. Muting and hidden tabs stop all sounds.
